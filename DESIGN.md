# Design System: ICG — Integrated Communications Group

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Campus Marquee"**

ICG is the campus marquee of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Pasig: an evergreen night board lit in marigold that tells you what is happening today, what is coming, and what already happened. The top of every page is loud and cinematic. That means a full-bleed photograph fading into evergreen night, condensed headlines set in capitals, and day numerals big enough to read across a room. Below that, the page turns calm and orderly. Light surfaces, even rows and clear dates make it read like an official record you can trust.

The system is built around dates. Every story and every event carries its day, and the calendar is never more than one click away. The "This week" strip of huge numerals under the hero is the signature element; the same numerals reappear in the calendar, the agenda and "On this day". The tone is energetic for students without being childish, and civic enough to be the university's record of itself.

It is flat and graphic. Depth comes from the change between night surfaces (the evergreen gradient) and day surfaces (sage mist and white), never from shadows. One accent, marigold, carries all the light in the system, so it is used sparingly.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Night at the top (hero, strip, page heads), day for reading (lists, cards, articles).
- One accent: marigold means "today", "the main action" or "selected".
- Anton in capitals only for loud moments: hero headlines, page titles, dates and numerals.
- Public Sans for everything people read or press.
- Real PLP photographs, never stock or illustration.
- Flat surfaces, pill-shaped controls, nearly square photos and panels.

## Colors

An evergreen-and-marigold palette: deep green-black night surfaces that deepen into forest green, one warm gold accent, and sage mist day surfaces in the same green family. It replaced the earlier Midnight (navy) palette at the client's request on 2026-11-15.

### Primary
- **Marquee Marigold** (`marigold`): the only accent. Today's day cell and today's calendar circle, the primary button, selected filter chips, links and labels on night surfaces, the "On this day" band. Hover on the primary button brightens it to **Lit Marigold** (`marigold-bright`). Text on marigold is always **Lamp Black** (`on-marigold`).

### Neutral — night surfaces
- **Evergreen Night** (`night`) into **Forest** (`night-end`): every night band is a 135° gradient from Evergreen Night (top left) to Forest (bottom right), defined once as `--gradient-night` and applied by the `bg-night` class: header, This week strip, page heads, night panels, process steps, footer. Photo fades and scrims use solid Evergreen Night (`--night-rgb`) so the hero melts into the strip below it without a seam.
- **Night Surface** (`surface`): hover on night surfaces.
- **Raised Night** (`surface-raised`): a selected day that is not today.
- **Night Line** (`line`): dividers and cell borders on night surfaces.
- **Marquee White** (`text-on-dark`): text on night surfaces. A warm off-white so it glows rather than glares.
- **Night Muted** (`muted-on-dark`): secondary text on night surfaces (dates, places, "No events").

### Neutral — day surfaces
- **Sage Mist** (`paper`): the page background for reading content, a pale green-gray tied to the night palette.
- **Card White** (`card`): panels, calendar cells, agenda cards. **Card Hover** (`card-hover`) for hovered cells.
- **Ink** (`ink`): text on day surfaces. A green-black close to Night, so night and day read as one family.
- **Ink Muted** (`ink-muted`): secondary text on day surfaces.
- **Ink Faint** (`ink-faint`): out-of-month day numbers only. It passes only for large text (3.5:1), so never use it for small text.
- **Amber Ink** (`amber`): links and category labels on day surfaces, because marigold is unreadable there.
- **Mist Rule** (`rule`): dividers and borders on day surfaces.
- **Disabled** (`disabled-bg`, `disabled-text`): disabled controls only.

### Status
- **Error Red** (`error`) and **Error Wash** (`error-soft`): errors only, meaning form error messages, invalid field borders, the error summary, and in the desk a failed load or a reply that couldn't be delivered. It is functional, not a second accent: never use it for decoration, categories or emphasis.

### Named Rules
**The One Marigold Rule.** Marigold means one of three things: today, the main action, or selected. If an element is none of those, it is not marigold. Category pills are neutral outlines for this reason.

**The Night Gradient Rule.** The Evergreen gradient is the only decorative gradient in the system. It appears on night bands and panels, always at 135° from `night` to `night-end`, never on text, buttons, cards on day surfaces or anything marigold. Photo fades are functional and use solid Evergreen Night.

**The Never on Paper Rule.** Marigold is never text on Mist or Card White (1.7:1). Use Amber Ink for links and labels on day surfaces, and keep marigold for fills there.

**The Contrast Floor Rule.** Body text is at least 4.5:1 against its surface; large text (24px and up, or 19px bold) at least 3:1. The verified pairs are listed at the top of `design/tokens.css`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Anton (fallback Arial Narrow, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Public Sans (fallback system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)

**Character:** Anton is a tall condensed face that reads like a cinema marquee or a poster date; Public Sans is a plain, civic sans built for government sites, so the reading layer stays calm and trustworthy. The pairing is loud headline over quiet record.

### Hierarchy
Every size on the site is one of the steps below; the token names are in the frontmatter. "→" lists the desktop size first, then smaller screens. If a page needs a size that isn't here, add a step here first.

- **Display, hero** (`display-hero` 84px → `display-hero-md` 60px → `display-hero-sm` 44px; Anton, line-height 0.96, capitals): the featured story or event headline in the hero. One per page.
- **Display, page** (`display-page` 112px → `display-page-md` 72px → `display-page-sm` 56px; Anton, capitals): page titles in the night band. Either a date ("NOVEMBER 2026") or the name of a record-keeping section ("THE ARCHIVE"). Keep them to three words or fewer.
- **Numerals** (Anton): `numeral-strip` 88px (week strip, desktop) → `numeral-time` 44px on mobile; `numeral-year` 72px → 44px for "On this day" years; `numeral-agenda` 64px → `numeral-agenda-sm` 40px for agenda dates; `numeral-time` 44px → `numeral-time-sm` 30px for event times; `numeral-cell` 26px → `numeral-cell-sm` 20px in calendar cells.
- **Headline, date** (`headline-date` 52px → `headline-date-sm` 30px; Anton, capitals): the date heading of a day's programme, such as "SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 14". The calendar day panel title uses `headline-panel` 40px → `headline-panel-sm` 32px.
- **Wordmark** (`wordmark` 40px → 30px in the header; `wordmark-footer` 56px → 44px; Anton): the "ICG" mark only.
- **Title, section** (`title-section` 28px → 22px; Public Sans 700, line-height 1.2): "Coming up", "Latest news". Feature sections such as "On this day" use `title-feature` 32px → `title-feature-sm` 24px. Headings inside night panels use 22px → `title-panel-sm` 20px.
- **Title, item** (Public Sans 700, line-height 1.3): event rows `title-item` 22px → `title-item-sm` 17px; news cards `title-news` 21px → `title-news-sm` 19px; day panel items 17px.
- **Body, lead** (`body-lead` 18px → `body` 16px; Public Sans 400, line-height 1.5): hero description, max 560px wide.
- **Body** (`body` 16px; Public Sans 400, line-height 1.5): running text, empty states. Keep lines between 65 and 75 characters.
- **Body, detail** (`body-detail` 15px, 500): event details, nav links, buttons, links.
- **Body, small** (`body-sm` 14px, 500): places, filter chips, secondary buttons.
- **Label** (`label` 13px → `label-sm` 12px; Public Sans 600, letter-spacing 0.06em, capitals): weekday names (MON, TUE) and "TODAY" only. Category pills use 12px semibold in sentence case.
- **Meta** (`meta` 13px; Public Sans 400): dates under items and footnotes.

**Implementation note:** define each type role once as a CSS class (for example `.t-hero`, `.t-section`, `.t-panel`) with its responsive steps, instead of repeating sizes on each element. The roles then hold even if a utility framework loads late, and pages can't drift from the scale.

### Named Rules
**The Loud Top, Calm Lists Rule.** Anton appears only in the hero headline, page titles, date headings and numerals (days, times, years). Section titles, item titles and everything people read are Public Sans. If Anton shows up in a paragraph, a button or a list item title, it's wrong.

**The No Eyebrow Rule.** Never put a small label above a heading. Category, place and date go under the title, in that item's meta line.

**The Plain Date Rule.** Write dates out in full: "November 14" (add the year in heroes and archives), "Saturday, November 14". Times are "9:00 AM", "12:00 NN", "4:00 PM". Ranges use "to" ("9:00 AM to 12:00 NN"), never a dash. Short weekday labels are three letters in capitals (MON).

## Layout

- **Container:** max 1440px wide. Side padding is 64px on desktop (1024px and up) and 16px on mobile.
- **Breakpoints:** mobile-first. 640px (`sm`) for two-up rows and inline button pairs, 1024px (`lg`) for the full desktop layout and the main navigation. Every page is checked at 390px and 1440px.
- **Night band, then day body.** Pages open with a night band (hero or page head), then switch to Mist for the content, then may close with the marigold "On this day" band and the night footer.
- **Home:** hero 720px tall on desktop and 760px on mobile, then the This week strip, then a two-column body: the programme (fluid) beside a 400px news column, with a 72px gap. On mobile everything stacks: programme first, then news, then announcements.
- **Calendar:** the month grid (fluid) beside a 380px day panel, with a 48px gap; the panel sticks below the header on desktop. On mobile the panel follows the grid.
- **Rhythm:** related items sit 8–16px apart; separate groups get 32–56px; sections get 56–72px of padding on desktop and 32–40px on mobile. There's more space above a heading than below it.
- **Rows, not card grids.** Lists of events and announcements are rows separated by thin rules. Cards are reserved for news with photos and for panels.

## Elevation & Depth

ICG is flat. There are no shadows anywhere. Depth comes from three things: the change between night and day surfaces, Card White panels sitting on Mist, and thin rules (1px Mist Rule on day, 1px Night Line on night). Photographs get depth from fades into solid Evergreen Night (`rgba(var(--night-rgb), …)`), never from drop shadows. Night bands carry the one decorative gradient (The Night Gradient Rule).

### Overlay Vocabulary
- **Hero fade, desktop:** a left-to-right Night fade (96% at the left edge, 86% at 34%, 45% at 58%, clear by 78%), plus a bottom fade from clear at 55% to solid Night at 100%, so the photo melts into the This week strip.
- **Hero fade, mobile:** a top-to-bottom Night fade (55% at the top behind the header, 25% at 16%, 70% at 34%, 94% at 50%, solid by 82%).
- **Hero soften:** a 7px blur on the photo, masked to the left 42–66% (bottom half on mobile), so posters and signage behind the headline stop competing with it.
- **Header scrim:** a 160px Night fade behind the transparent header (78% → 35% → clear). After 40px of scroll the header takes the Evergreen gradient at 94% opacity with a 10px backdrop blur and a 1px Night Line under it.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Marquee Rule.** No box shadows, no glows, no glassy cards. If something needs to stand apart, change its surface (night/day/card) or add a 1px rule.

## Shapes

- **Pills (999px)** for everything you press: buttons, filter chips, category pills, icon buttons, the Month/Agenda switch, today's circle in the calendar.
- **Nearly square (6px)** for photos, panels, news cards and agenda cards.
- **Small (4px)** for the event chips inside calendar cells, and for focus ring corners.
- **Square (0)** for structural bands and grid cells: hero, strip cells, calendar grid cells (the grid itself has a 6px outer radius).
- **Borders:** 1px only. Selected states use a 2px inset outline (Ink on day surfaces, Marigold on night surfaces).

**The Pressable Outline Rule.** On day surfaces, a pill with a 1px outline is something you press (buttons, filter chips, pagination, topic choices). Things that only report a state, such as status badges and counts, are filled and borderless, and are smaller (24px) than the smallest button (44px).
- **Indicators:** the active nav link has a 22×3px marigold bar under it; the hero carousel uses 3px dashes (40px for the current slide, 20px for the rest).

## Components

Flat and graphic: pill controls, rows over cards, one marigold light per view.

### Public navigation — connected design preview
- Public pages share Home, Calendar, Archive, About, and Contact navigation. News is available through the Archive's News filter; events are available through the Calendar and its Agenda view. There are no separate News or Events listing pages. Story and event links retain matching details, and calendar links retain the selected date.
- The header is 76px on mobile and 92px from 1024px, with 16px/64px page gutters. Keep the compact ICG wordmark, search, and a 44px menu button on mobile. Desktop shows the five section links and a pill Log in action; show the full institution lockup when there is room at 1440px. One section has the existing 22×3px Marigold active indicator.
- Mobile navigation uses a full-height Night dialog, sentence-case Public Sans links, a 44px close button, focus containment, Escape-to-close, and focus return to the menu button. Do not animate the dialog. Keep the underlying page from scrolling while it is open.
- The common footer carries the ICG identity line, section links, the campus address, and Contact/Submit an event links. The local presentation is labeled “Design preview · Sample campus content.” Omit unconfirmed social destinations instead of inventing URLs.
- Archive type controls may scroll horizontally on narrow phones; they must not widen the document. Calendar downloads produce a Philippine Time snapshot, labeled “Download calendar,” rather than implying a connected subscription service.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** fully rounded pill, 48px tall (44px minimum anywhere).
- **Primary:** Marquee Marigold with Lamp Black bold text, 28px side padding. One per view, for the main action ("See event details", "Read the story", "Subscribe to this calendar"). Hover: Lit Marigold. Disabled: Disabled colors.
- **Secondary on night:** transparent, Marquee White text, 1px border at 60% white; hover adds an 8% white wash. Often carries an icon ("Add to calendar").
- **On marigold:** Ink fill with Mist text (hover: Night Surface), or Ink outline. See the call-to-action band.
- **Secondary on day:** transparent, Ink text, 1px Ink border; hover fills Ink with Mist text ("See the full November calendar", "Add to calendar" in panels).
- **Icon button:** 44×44px circle, transparent, 20–22px icon. Bordered (40% white) when it changes data (previous/next week, previous/next month); borderless when it moves through a carousel or opens search and menu.
- **Focus:** 2px marigold outline with a 3px offset on night surfaces, 2px Ink outline on day surfaces.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open every page with a night band and set the reading content on Mist or Card White.
- **Do** show a date on every story and event, and link to the calendar from every page.
- **Do** keep marigold to today, the one primary action per view, and selected filters (The One Marigold Rule).
- **Do** use Amber Ink for links on day surfaces and marigold for links on night surfaces.
- **Do** make every tap target at least 44×44px and keep at least 8px between targets.
- **Do** give every control hover, focus, selected and disabled states, with the focus rings described above.
- **Do** write sentence-case copy in plain words, and name buttons by their action ("Add to calendar", not "Submit").
- **Do** respect reduced motion: crossfades (500ms, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) and hover transitions (150–250ms) switch off.
- **Do** check every page at 390px and 1440px before calling it done.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put Anton in buttons, paragraphs, nav links or item titles (The Loud Top, Calm Lists Rule).
- **Don't** put a label or kicker above a heading (The No Eyebrow Rule).
- **Don't** use marigold as text on Mist or Card White.
- **Don't** add box shadows, glows, glassy cards or gradient text (The Flat Marquee Rule), and don't use any gradient other than the night gradient and photo fades.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent color, or color-code categories. Error Red is for errors only.
- **Don't** put a boxed card inside another card. Group with spacing, dividers and subheadings instead.
- **Don't** use the bright PLP green (#1d5c3a) or the old Midnight navy, warm cream backgrounds or the old broadsheet look; those directions were tried and replaced. The green lives only in the deep Evergreen night surfaces.
- **Don't** use Lundayan branding anywhere, or lorem ipsum.
- **Don't** copy Manila Bulletin's masthead, logo or colors. It is a reference for the concept (a news and events record), not for the look.
- **Don't** autoplay the hero carousel or give its arrows the same bordered style as the week buttons.
