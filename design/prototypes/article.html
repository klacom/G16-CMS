ICG summit asks every college to tell its own story

More than 300 student journalists, organization officers and faculty filled the auditorium for the first campus-wide communications summit.

Campus By the ICG news desk November 12, 2026 4 minute read

Share

The Integrated Communications Group held its first campus-wide communications summit on Thursday, bringing together student journalists, organization officers and faculty advisers from every college.

The day was built around one request: that each college start reporting its own events, so the university's record of what happens on campus is complete. ICG will publish what the colleges send in, with dates attached, so every event ends up on the shared calendar and in the archive.

Why the colleges, and why now

Until now, most campus news reached students through scattered social media pages. Events were announced, held and then hard to find a month later. Organizers said the new site is meant to fix that by keeping one dated record that anyone can search by day, month or year.

Workshops in the afternoon covered writing a short event report, taking photos people can use, and getting consent before photographing students. Each college council left with a reporting guide and a contact on the ICG desk.

What changes for students

Students will see college events on the home page as they are confirmed, and can add any event to their phone's calendar with one tap. Past events stay in the archive, linked from the day they happened.

Organizations that want an event listed can send the date, time, place and a short description through the contact form. The ICG desk reviews every submission before it is published.
